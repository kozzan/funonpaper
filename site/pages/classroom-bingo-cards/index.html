Sight word bingo cards for the classroom

Practise sight words or new vocabulary with a different bingo card for every student. Start from the Dolch lists or paste in this week’s words.

How to make your cards

  1. Use the word list as it is, edit it, or paste your own: one word or short phrase per line, at least 24.
  2. Choose how many cards you need (one per player, plus a few spares) and how many go on each page.
  3. Pick US Letter or A4, then press Print or save as PDF. To get a PDF, choose Save as PDF as the printer.
  4. Keep the set code. Typing it in again later gives you exactly the same cards, so you can print more copies or check a winning card against the original.

Playing sight word bingo in class

Give every student a card and some counters. Read a word aloud, or show it on a flash card, and give students a moment to find it. Tick it off on the caller sheet. The first student with five in a row reads the five words back to the class, which is where most of the practice happens.

The pre-primer list has 40 words and the primer list 52. Pick a list under Start from a list, or mix them by pasting both into the box.

Using your own vocabulary

Paste any list with at least 24 different entries: spelling words, a foreign-language vocabulary list, maths facts written as answers, or science terms. For definitions bingo, put the words on the cards and read out the definitions.

Frequently asked questions

Which sight word lists are included?

The Dolch pre-primer (40 words) and primer (52 words) lists.

How many cards do I need for a class?

One per student. With 30 students, set 30 cards at 2 or 4 per page. Every card is different, so neighbours cannot copy.

Can I print the same cards next term?

Yes. Write down the set code and type it in again with the same word list to get identical cards.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no watermark, and you can print as many cards as you like.

Can I save the cards as a PDF?

Yes. Press Print or save as PDF and choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer. The PDF can then be emailed or printed somewhere else.

Is what I type sent anywhere?

No. The cards are made in your browser. Your word list never leaves your device.
