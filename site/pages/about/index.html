About Fun on Paper

Fun on Paper makes games you set up on a screen and play on paper: at a party, in a classroom, or around the kitchen table. The first is bingo, with number cards, 90-ball tickets and themed word cards. More printables will follow.

How it works

Everything is made in your browser. When you press print, your browser lays out the pages and can save them as a PDF. Nothing you type, such as a word list, is sent to us or stored anywhere.

Each set of cards is worked out from its set code. The same code, card type and word list always give the same cards, so a set can be printed again or a winning card checked.

Free to use

The games are free for home, school and office use. The site may show advertising in the future to pay for itself; the cards you print will never carry ads.
