Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. The site has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics, and it sets no cookies. The bingo cards are made by your own browser, and what you type into the forms, such as a word list or a title, never leaves your device. A link you copy from the address bar contains your card settings and set code, but not your word list.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub records technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, to run and protect the service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for how it handles that data.

Advertising

The site does not show ads today. If that changes, this page will be updated first to say which ad provider is used, what it collects and how you can say no to personalised ads.

Questions

Email hello@funonpaper.com. If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you. See also the contact page and about Fun on Paper.
