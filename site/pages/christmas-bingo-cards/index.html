How to make your cards

  1. Use the word list as it is, edit it, or paste your own: one word or short phrase per line, at least 24.
  2. Choose how many cards you need (one per player, plus a few spares) and how many go on each page.
  3. Pick US Letter or A4, then press Print or save as PDF. To get a PDF, choose Save as PDF as the printer.
  4. Keep the set code. Typing it in again later gives you exactly the same cards, so you can print more copies or check a winning card against the original.

How to play Christmas bingo

Hand out one card per player. The caller reads out the Christmas words one at a time, in any order, and ticks each one off on the caller sheet. Players mark the word if it is on their card. The first to get five in a row across, down or diagonally calls “Bingo!”.

For younger children, cut the caller sheet into slips and let them take turns drawing from a hat. For a longer game, play to a full card.

Ideas for the words

The list has 48 Christmas words. Delete any you do not want, or add your own: family traditions, names of the people at the table, or things you will see during the day, such as a film everyone watches. A card needs at least 24 different words.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the Christmas words?

Yes. Edit the list in the box: one word or short phrase per line. The cards update as you type.

What can we use as markers?

Anything small: chocolate coins, sweets, buttons or a pen.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no watermark, and you can print as many cards as you like.

Can I save the cards as a PDF?

Yes. Press Print or save as PDF and choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer. The PDF can then be emailed or printed somewhere else.

Is what I type sent anywhere?

No. The cards are made in your browser. Your word list never leaves your device.
