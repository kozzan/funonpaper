Baby shower bingo cards

Gift bingo keeps guests busy while the presents are opened. These cards come filled with baby gift words, and you can change any of them.

How to make your cards

  1. Use the word list as it is, edit it, or paste your own: one word or short phrase per line, at least 24.
  2. Choose how many cards you need (one per player, plus a few spares) and how many go on each page.
  3. Pick US Letter or A4, then press Print or save as PDF. To get a PDF, choose Save as PDF as the printer.
  4. Keep the set code. Typing it in again later gives you exactly the same cards, so you can print more copies or check a winning card against the original.

How to play baby shower bingo

Give each guest a card as they arrive. As the parent-to-be opens the presents, guests mark any gift on their card. The first guest with five in a row across, down or diagonally calls “Bingo!” and wins a small prize. Keep playing for a second winner or a full card.

If the gifts run out before anyone wins, the host can read out the rest of the words from the caller sheet.

Make it your own

The list has 45 common baby gifts. Remove anything that does not suit, or add items from the gift registry so the cards match what is actually being opened. A card needs at least 24 different words.

Frequently asked questions

Can guests fill in their own cards?

Not with this maker: it fills every square from the list. To make the game closer to the gifts, use items from the registry as the list.

How long does a game take?

About as long as the present opening, which is the point. With more gifts than words on a card, someone usually wins well before the end.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no watermark, and you can print as many cards as you like.

Can I save the cards as a PDF?

Yes. Press Print or save as PDF and choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer. The PDF can then be emailed or printed somewhere else.

Is what I type sent anywhere?

No. The cards are made in your browser. Your word list never leaves your device.
